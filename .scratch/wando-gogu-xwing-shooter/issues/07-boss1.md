# Design the Stage 1 boss fight

Type: grilling
Blocked by: 02, 04

## Question

What is the Stage 1 boss fight for the X-wing shooter (see [map.md](../map.md))?

Blocked on the stage-themes ticket ([02-stages.md](02-stages.md), boss should fit stage 1's setting) and the Gogu-skills ticket ([04-gogu-skills.md](04-gogu-skills.md), the boss's win-mechanic may lean on a Gogu skill). The user's explicit requirement: every boss needs a distinct, unique win-mechanic — a weak spot, attrition via massive HP, or a mechanic tied to a Gogu skill — and no two bosses should be won the same way, so check what [08-boss2.md](08-boss2.md) and [09-boss3.md](09-boss3.md) land on (if already resolved) to avoid repeating a mechanic. Kid-friendly: no blood or gore.

Resolve:
- What is the boss, visually/thematically, for stage 1?
- What is its unique win-mechanic (pick one lane: weak spot, massive-HP attrition, or Gogu-skill-dependent — and be specific about how it plays out)?
- What attack patterns does it have, and how do they escalate/phase as the fight progresses?
- As stage 1's boss, this should likely be the gentlest of the three — establish that relative difficulty.

Work it live with the user, one question at a time. Pair with `/prototype` if a quick rough mockup of the attack pattern would sharpen the discussion.
