# Pick the 3 stage themes & settings

Type: grilling

## Question

What are the 3 stage themes/settings for the X-wing shooter (see [map.md](../map.md))?

The user gave full creative freedom on world/enemy design, with one hard constraint: kid-friendly, no blood or gore. Pick 3 distinct pseudo-Star-Wars settings (e.g. desert canyon, ice field, asteroid belt, space station interior — these are only examples, not a prescription) that:

- Escalate naturally in visual intensity/difficulty from stage 1 to stage 3.
- Each give enough identity to hang a distinct enemy roster and a distinct boss on (those are separate tickets — this ticket only fixes the theme/setting and enough flavor to brief those tickets).
- Fit a 1942-style vertical/horizontal scroller — i.e. a setting that reads clearly while scrolling past at speed.

Work it live with the user, one question at a time. Record the 3 themes (with a one-line visual/flavor description each) as the answer.
